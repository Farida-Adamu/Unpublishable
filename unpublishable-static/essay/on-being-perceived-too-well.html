On Being Perceived Too Well

The danger is no longer being misread. It's being read exactly.

The old anxiety was that nobody understood you. The new anxiety is that several strangers understand you with uncomfortable precision, that they have collated your posts and your purchases and your face, and that they could write your eulogy from memory by Thursday.

Being perceived too well is its own kind of erasure. There is no room left to surprise yourself. The recommendation engine has already surprised you on your behalf, gently, in the direction of more of what you have already done.

I think this is what people mean, vaguely, when they say they want to log off. They don't want privacy in the legal sense. They want the right to be slightly mysterious to themselves.
