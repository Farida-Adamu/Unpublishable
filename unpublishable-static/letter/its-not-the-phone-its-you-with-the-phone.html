It's Not the Phone. It's You With the Phone.

My mother has been telling me, for roughly fifteen years, that the problem is the damn phone. I have been telling her, for roughly fifteen years, that it is more complicated than that. We have both been right and both been losing.

The complication I have been defending is real: the phone is a vector for many things, not all of them bad, and blaming the device is a way of not blaming the system or the self. Fine. I still believe this. I also believe, increasingly, that my mother is closer to the truth than any of us in the discourse have been willing to admit, because admitting it would cost us our subject matter.

The thing is not the phone. The thing is what the phone is for. The thing is what we have agreed, collectively, to be the kind of person who carries one. Mom: you were right about the symptom. I will keep being annoying about the cause.

Yours, still logged in — The Editor
