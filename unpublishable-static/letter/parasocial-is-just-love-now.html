Parasocial Is Just Love Now

I want to make a small, unpopular claim: parasocial is no longer a useful word, because it describes essentially every form of attachment a person under forty currently has access to. The category has eaten the world it was meant to describe.

A friend you see twice a year and follow daily is a parasocial friend. A coworker whose Slack avatar you know better than their handwriting is parasocial. The person you're dating, who you text more than you touch, is — and I'm sorry — at least a little parasocial. The word was supposed to mark a deviation. It now marks the baseline.

Older relatives are clearer-eyed about this than we are. My mother, who reads two newspapers and watches one anchor, calls the anchor by his first name. She knows it's a one-way street. We pretend ours are two-way because we get to type back.

Yours, still logged in — The Editor
