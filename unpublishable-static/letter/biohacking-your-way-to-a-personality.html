Biohacking Your Way to a Personality

There is a man I follow — and I follow him in the parasocial sense established above — who has, through a combination of sleep tracking and saunas and a kind of devotional protein intake, optimized himself into a person with nothing left to say. He posts about the protocol. The protocol is the personality.

I do not begrudge him his magnesium. I begrudge the cultural settlement in which the cultivation of a self has been replaced by the maintenance of a self, in which we have agreed that what you do is less interesting than what you avoid.

An old self-help book asks: what would you do if you knew you couldn't fail? A new one asks: what is your morning routine? These are very different questions.

Yours, still logged in — The Editor
