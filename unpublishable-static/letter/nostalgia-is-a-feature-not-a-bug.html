Nostalgia Is a Feature, Not a Bug

We are now nostalgic for things that have not yet finished happening. The 2010s revival began in 2022. The pandemic-era aesthetic was being mourned while the pandemic was still, technically, on. The cycle has compressed to the point that the past tense is now a marketing tense.

I don't blame the algorithm for this, exactly. I blame the absence of new shared objects. When there's no present worth pointing at, the safest pointer is backward, to a thing your audience has already agreed to like.

The future used to be a place the culture imagined. Now it's a slot the culture leaves empty so it can sell you the past again next quarter.
