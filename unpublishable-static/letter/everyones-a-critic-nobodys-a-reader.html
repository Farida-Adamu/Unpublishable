Everyone's a Critic, Nobody's a Reader

There is a particular kind of book review you can now write without reading the book. I have written several. So have you, probably, in the form of a retweet. The review is of the reception, not the work. We are all in the second-derivative business.

This isn't a complaint about laziness — laziness is fine, laziness is human. It's a complaint about a feedback loop in which the original object becomes optional. The novel, the film, the essay: they're now occasions for a discourse that has already decided what it thinks.

The text, if it has any teeth, eventually bites back. But only for the few who go and look.

Yours, still logged in — The Editor
